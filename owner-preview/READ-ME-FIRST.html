JR Roofing Intelligence

The real application — free Windows owner review.

Start in three steps

  1. Extract the ZIP. Right-click the downloaded ZIP, choose Extract All, then open the extracted folder. Do not launch from inside the ZIP.
  2. Double-click START JR Roofing.cmd. A console window will prepare your fictional projects and open your normal browser. Keep that console window open.
  3. Double-click SHOW LOGIN DETAILS.cmd. Copy the owner username/password into the app login page. Passwords are generated privately on your computer; none is preset in the download.

Windows may hide the .cmd part of a filename. Look for START JR Roofing with type “Windows Command Script”.

Node.js is included. You do not need to install Node, npm, Git, Python, developer tools or any paid service. After downloading, the package runs offline on Windows 10/11 64-bit. Use Edge or Chrome.

What to explore

Choose the fictional Riverside Warehouse project in Active project. A second fictional project, Meadow Offices, is also included.

  • Survey: two roofs, a defect and a clearly labelled fictional illustration. Add observations and photos. Download the existing draft survey PDF or create another draft.
  • Materials: a sourced fictional product and rate. Edit a revision to see history.
  • Estimating: two options, category costs and VAT. Option A initially totals £5,775 including VAT. Download its draft quotation PDF.
  • Structural Screening / Design: the 18 mm OSB input, drawing revision, load case, query and RFI remain pending review.
  • Construction: programme, diary, draft RAMS/CPP, scaffold, working-at-height and hot-work controls.
  • Quality / Handover: inspection hold points, an open snag and draft O&M references. Missing approvals block release and closeout.
  • V3.6 Original Workspace: the recovered original app is preserved. Its browser-local records are separate from the newer backend database.

Testing approval roles

SHOW LOGIN DETAILS lists separate fictional accounts: owner (manager), reviewer (independent manager), engineer, supervisor and viewer (read-only).

Sign out before switching accounts. For example, owner cannot approve a document they authored; sign in as reviewer to test independent review. An engineer account alone does not allow structural acceptance: a project appointment and current evidence are still required.

Fictional software testing only. No engineer appointment, structural acceptance, inspection acceptance or work release is pre-approved. These sample roles do not establish real competence. Never use these projects as site permission or safety certification.

Your work stays on your computer

The launcher opens a loopback address beginning http://127.0.0.1:. It is accessible only on your computer. There is no public deployment, subscription, AI generation, email service or payment setup.

Your changes persist in the ReviewData folder. Reopen the same extracted folder next time. Do not delete ReviewData or replace it with a fresh copy if you want to keep your work. Login details are encrypted for your Windows user account.

Use BACK UP REVIEW DATA.cmd for a consistent, verified database copy. Backups are saved in ReviewData\Backups. Keep the whole review folder private. The V3.6 browser-local workspace has its own Export project data function; its records are not included in the backend database backup.

To stop, close the JR Roofing console window or press Ctrl+C in it. Closing only the browser tab leaves the local app running.

If something does not open

  • Confirm you used Extract All and kept all folders together.
  • If the browser does not open automatically, copy the http://127.0.0.1:... address shown in the console into Edge or Chrome.
  • If Windows or company security blocks a file, do not disable antivirus, SmartScreen or company policies. Share the exact message for help.
  • Keep the original JR Roofing console open. If it says the app is already running, use that existing window/tab.
  • This is a Windows x64 package. It does not run directly on an iPhone, Mac or Chromebook. It is intentionally not shared across your Wi-Fi network.
