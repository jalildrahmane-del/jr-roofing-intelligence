JR Roofing Intelligence V3.6

Extract the entire ZIP into one folder. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge or Safari. Keep roof-drone.mp4 and roof-poster.jpg alongside index.html.

The video is muted and loops without controls. The app workspace is the V3.5 baseline; no application modules were removed.

For more reliable playback, serve the folder over localhost using: python -m http.server 8000
Then open http://localhost:8000 in your browser.
